E-commerce churn risk

A current reconstruction evaluates a churn classifier on a stratified holdout and makes the precision/recall decision visible.

Question

How well can a model identify customers labeled as churned in the supplied e-commerce dataset?

Evidence

5,630 records · 1,126 holdout records · ROC AUC 0.887

Read the case

What the analysis shows

The model improves ranking over the majority baseline, while its 0.5 threshold detects 42.1% of churn cases at 83.3% precision. A different threshold changes that trade-off. No retention intervention or business impact was measured.
