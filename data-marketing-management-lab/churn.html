E-commerce churn risk

Can customer behavior help flag churn risk on unseen records? This current reconstruction tests a simple, auditable classifier against a majority-class baseline.

Data and method

The supplied E Comm sheet contains 5,630 records and 18 candidate behavior/profile fields. CustomerID is excluded. A fixed, stratified 80/20 split reserves 1,126 records for testing. Missing-value imputation, category encoding and scaling are fitted on the training split only.

  1. Validate target and unique customer IDs
  2. Split by target class; hold out 20%
  3. Fit preprocessing on training rows
  4. Compare baseline and regularized logistic model

The model uses L2-regularized logistic regression with full-batch gradient descent in NumPy. The default decision threshold is 0.5.

Interpretation and limits

At the default threshold, flagged users are often churn cases, but the model misses 116 of 190 churn cases. A retention decision would need an agreed cost for missed churn versus unnecessary outreach, and threshold selection on a separate validation set.

This is one holdout on one supplied dataset. It does not show causal drivers, campaign lift, or production performance. Source data is not bundled; use only an authorized local copy to rerun.
