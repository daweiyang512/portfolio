E-commerce churn risk

A held-out classification analysis that makes the baseline, evaluation split, and threshold trade-off inspectable.

Data and method

The supplied Original dataset sheet contains 5,630 records. The customer identifier, derived Churnclass, and old prediction/confidence columns are excluded. A fixed-seed, stratified 80/20 split keeps 4,504 records for training and 1,126 for testing. Imputation, one-hot encoding, and scaling are fitted on training data only.

The model is L2-regularized logistic regression. It is compared with a majority-class baseline at a declared probability threshold of 0.5.

At threshold 0.5, the model identifies 80 of 190 churn cases in the holdout and flags 96 records as at risk. Lowering the threshold raises recall and lowers precision; see the threshold table.

Interpretation and limits

This is one holdout on one supplied dataset. It does not establish production performance, causal drivers, or the impact of a retention campaign. A deployment threshold would need business-cost validation and further testing.

Identifiers and derived target fields are excluded from the model features. The analysis keeps all source rows local; the public repository contains aggregate evaluation evidence only.
