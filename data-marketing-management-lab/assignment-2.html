Assignment 2 · Descriptive text analytics

Brand conversations and engagement

A current descriptive analysis of 324 public social posts across Amazon, PayPal, Walmart and UPS, with an explicit check that source posts match the supplied topic labels.

Input integrity

324Posts in source
4Brand accounts
6Distinct values in numeric ID field
1:1Match by UTC timestamp + full text

The numeric post ID column is not safe as a unique key in the supplied file: it has only six distinct values for 324 rows. The rebuild verifies a one-to-one match using normalized UTC timestamps and exact post text instead.

  1. Read source posts and supplied labels
  2. Normalize timestamps to UTC
  3. Validate unique timestamp/text pairs
  4. Aggregate by brand, label and month

Topic mix by brand

Labels already present in the supplied classification file: 205 informational and 119 emotional. The chart shows the emotional share within each brand's posts.

UPS has the highest emotional-label share in this sample. This is a descriptive difference in the supplied labels; it does not establish audience preference or campaign effectiveness.

Engagement and scope

The analysis also reports mean and median likes, replies and retweets by brand and topic, plus monthly topic counts. Medians help show typical observed engagement when a few posts have unusually high counts.

The sample is small and observational. Engagement counts are not adjusted for impressions or account size. Topic labels are treated as input because their original generation method is not reproduced in this package.
