Independent analysis · Baja Sardinia, Sardinia

Positioning a coastal villa in its market

An owner-requested analysis that connects a regional listing market, a local comparison set, and a probability-weighted rental model to frame a practical hold, rent, or sell decision.

Market scope

966 canonical listings

715 records had an asking price; 595 were in the calibrated core-market segment used for the final price-position analysis.

Subject position

€620k · lower-half core

About the 24.5th percentile by asking price and the 37th percentile by asking price per usable sqm in the final reference distributions.

Decision lens

Market × operations

Rental capacity is modeled by month and operating structure; a sell decision remains open without a verified alternative use of capital.

Question

Where does the subject villa sit in the wider Costa Smeralda residential market, how does the nearby Baja Sardinia / Mucchi Bianchi market change that comparison, and what does a seasonal rental model imply after operating costs?

The work is a market-reference and decision-support case. Asking prices are not verified transaction prices, modeled rent is not realized income, and the analysis is not an independent appraisal.

Method

1 · Build the market frame

Collect and canonicalize regional listings, separate price regimes, then use a calibrated core-market subset rather than letting luxury-tail observations define the benchmark.

2 · Locate the asset

Compare asking price and price per sqm with the final core-market distributions. Use an exploratory recursive screen to rank property form, location, and area; interpret the ranking as separation, not causality.

3 · Add local and rental evidence

Use a deduplicated local listing universe and a 12-month probability-weighted rental model. Keep observed local rent benchmarks visibly separate from modeled subject-asset rents.

4 · Test the decision boundary

Compare operating structures after the base fixed-cost layer and stress the income assumptions. Do not claim that holding dominates selling when alternative capital returns are unknown.

Limits and quality controls

  • Market segmentation labels are analytical working categories, not official classifications.
  • The local conclusion depends on the selected deduplication and inclusion rules. The case keeps that sensitivity visible instead of treating one local discount as universal.
  • Rental figures are probability-weighted model outputs. They are not booking history or guaranteed revenue.
  • The structural ranking is exploratory. It does not identify causal price effects.
